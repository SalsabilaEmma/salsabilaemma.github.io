Digital Auto Addict 99

Portfolio / Video Editor & AI Content Producer

Overview

Digital Auto Addict 99 is a YouTube channel focused on automotive content, produced entirely using AI-driven workflows. I manage the full content production pipeline — from generating visual assets using AI tools, to video editing, voiceover, and publishing-ready output. I started as a freelance video editor (Aug 2025) and transitioned to full-time as my responsibilities expanded.

My Role

As Video Editor & AI Content Producer, my responsibilities include:

  • Writing AI prompts to generate visual assets using Google Flow (Veo) — from image generation to AI video generation
  • Editing AI-generated video assets into publish-ready YouTube content — pacing, visual narrative structure, and branding consistency
  • Writing narrative scripts with Claude AI assistance
  • Integrating scripts with Google Text-to-Speech to produce voice-overs suited to the channel's tone
  • Writing SEO-oriented YouTube descriptions and titles to improve content discoverability
Tools Used

Google Flow (Veo) Claude AI Google Text-to-Speech Video Editing AI Prompt Engineering YouTube SEO

Challenge & Solution

The main challenge was building a consistent, efficient AI content pipeline from scratch — balancing video quality, tone consistency, and production speed. This was solved by developing a structured prompt engineering workflow for Google Flow (Veo) and standardizing the script-to-voiceover process using Claude AI and Google TTS, resulting in a repeatable, scalable production cycle.
